Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about CarTools applications, privacy, features, and compatibility.

What is CarTools?
CarTools is a collection of intelligent web applications designed to help drivers manage their vehicles more efficiently. From fuel consumption tracking to gig driver profit analysis, every tool is built to turn driving data into practical insights.
Which applications are available?

CarTools currently offers two applications:

  • Fuel Analytics — Monitor fuel consumption, vehicle health, and driving efficiency.
  • GIG DRIVER — Track income, expenses, and true profitability for rideshare and delivery drivers.

More tools are planned as the platform continues to grow.

Do I need to install anything?
No. CarTools is completely web-based. Simply open the application in your browser and start using it—no installation required.
Can I use CarTools on my phone?
Yes. Every CarTools application is fully responsive and optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices, providing a smooth experience wherever you are.
Is my data private?
Yes. Protecting your privacy is a priority. Many features store data locally in your browser, giving you control over your information while minimizing unnecessary data collection.
Which countries are supported?
CarTools is designed for drivers worldwide. Applications support multiple measurement systems, including Metric, US Imperial, and UK units, with automatic region detection where available.
Who is CarTools built for?

CarTools is designed for:

  • Everyday drivers who want to reduce fuel costs
  • Car enthusiasts interested in vehicle performance
  • Rideshare and delivery drivers
  • Small fleet owners
  • Anyone looking to better understand the real cost of driving
Will more tools be added?
Yes. CarTools is an expanding platform, and new applications focused on vehicle management, maintenance, driving analytics, and cost optimization are planned for future releases.
Do I need technical knowledge to use CarTools?
Not at all. Every application is designed with a clean, intuitive interface that makes advanced analytics accessible to everyone—from casual drivers to professionals.
Why choose CarTools?
Unlike traditional vehicle tracking apps, CarTools focuses on turning raw driving data into meaningful insights. Our goal is to help you make smarter decisions, reduce operating costs, improve vehicle performance, and maximize profitability through intelligent analytics and modern user experiences.
