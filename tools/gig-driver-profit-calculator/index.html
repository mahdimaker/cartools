Uber, Lyft, and DoorDash show you a big number on your direct deposit. But every mile you drive takes money straight out of your pocket in 4 hidden ways:

Fuel & Energy Drain

15% - 25% of Gross Income

Rising pump prices and idling in drive-thrus silently consume a quarter of every payout. What looked like a $12 order turns into $8.50 before you even park.

Vehicle Equity Drop

$0.15 - $0.22 per Mile Driven

Every 1,000 gig miles permanently drops your car's resale value and accelerates tire wear, brake pads, transmission fluid, and suspension repair cycles.

Unpaid "Dead-Time"

25% - 35% of Online Hours

Apps only compensate you when on an active trip. Circling downtown waiting for pings or driving home empty-handed cuts your real hourly rate in half.

15.3% FICA + Income Taxes

As a 1099 independent contractor, you owe both employee and employer taxes. Without an IRS-compliant mileage audit log, you hand thousands in unneeded taxes to the government.
