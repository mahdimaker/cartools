Help & Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Fuel Analyzer, calculations, measurement systems, privacy, and vehicle tracking.

How many refuels do I need before Fuel Analyzer starts calculating fuel consumption?
You only need two complete refueling records. After your second full refuel, Fuel Analyzer has enough information to calculate your real fuel economy and unlock advanced analytics throughout the dashboard.
Do I need to fill the tank completely every time?
No. Partial refuels are fully supported and can still be recorded. However, full tank refills provide the most accurate fuel consumption calculations.
Can I manage more than one vehicle?
Yes. Fuel Analyzer supports multiple vehicles. Each vehicle has its own profile, fuel history, analytics, reports, and settings, so your data always stays organized.
Which measurement systems are supported?

Fuel Analyzer automatically supports drivers worldwide.

Available unit systems include:

  • Metric (L/100km, km/L, kilometers)
  • US MPG (Miles per Gallon)
  • UK Imperial MPG

You can switch between measurement systems at any time.

Is my data stored online?

No.

All fuel records are stored locally inside your browser. Your information stays on your device and is never uploaded unless you choose to export it yourself.

Do I need to create an account?

No registration is required.

Simply open the app, create a vehicle profile, and start tracking your fuel history immediately.

Can I export my fuel history?

Yes.

Your complete fuel log can be exported for backup, reporting, or transferring to another device. Existing data can also be imported whenever needed.

How accurate are the calculations?
Fuel Analyzer uses your actual odometer readings and refueling history instead of manufacturer estimates, providing calculations that closely reflect your real driving conditions.
What can the AI assistant do?
The AI assistant reviews your fuel history, driving patterns, and vehicle performance to provide personalized recommendations that help improve fuel economy, reduce fuel costs, and identify unusual changes in efficiency.
Is Fuel Analyzer free to use?
Yes. Fuel Analyzer is available as a free web application with no registration required.
