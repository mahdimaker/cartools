How to Reduce Roof Drag & Save Fuel

Simple structural changes can drastically reduce your fuel bill on your next road trip.

1

Remove Empty Racks

Take off empty crossbars when not in use to save up to 11% fuel.

2

Use Rear Hitch Racks

Rear hitch bike mounts create ~70% less aerodynamic drag than roof mounts.

3

Lower Cruising Speed

Cruising at 65 mph instead of 75 mph cuts roof drag overhead by nearly half!
