Frequently Asked Questions

Gig Driver FAQ

Everything you need to know about tracking rideshare and delivery earnings completely free.

Is Gig Driver completely free to use?

Yes, Gig Driver is 100% free. There are no premium subscriptions, hidden charges, or credit card requirements. You get full, unrestricted access to all shift logging, expense calculations, and performance analytics tools at zero cost.

How does Gig Driver calculate my true net profit?

Gig Driver calculates your true net profit by deducting hidden operating expenses from your gross earnings. It factors in your vehicle’s specific fuel consumption, real-time fuel prices, mileage depreciation (wear-and-tear), tolls, parking, and custom shift expenses to reveal your actual take-home pay.

Do I need a live GPS connection to track my mileage with Gig Driver?

No, Gig Driver is a lightweight, privacy-focused manual shift and expense log. Instead of draining your battery with continuous live GPS tracking, you simply input your shift distance (miles or kilometers) to instantly compute your fuel costs, depreciation, and net earnings per mile.

Can I use Gig Driver for multiple delivery and rideshare apps at the same time?

Absolutely. Gig Driver is built for multi-app drivers. You can track shift data and compare net earnings across major platforms like Uber, Lyft, DoorDash, UberEats, Instacart, Amazon Flex, and local courier services to see which app pays you the highest true net hourly rate.

How does vehicle depreciation calculation work in the app?

Through the Calibration settings, you can customize your vehicle profile (Gasoline, Hybrid, EV, or Diesel) and set your estimated depreciation cost per mile/km. The app then automatically deducts this vehicle wear-and-tear expense from every shift you log.

Can I export my shift logs for tax reporting and deductions?

Yes! You can export your full shift history, mileage records, and logged expenses into a structured CSV file anytime. This provides a clean audit trail of your driving activities and business expenses to share with your accountant or use during tax preparation.

Is my financial shift data secure?

Yes, your data belongs entirely to you. Gig Driver processes and retains your shift information securely, and you can easily back up, import, or export all your logs and settings to a CSV file whenever you need.

Does Gig Driver support both Metric and Imperial systems?

Yes! Gig Driver fully supports Metric, Imperial, and UK measurement systems. Whether you calculate your efficiency in Miles/Gallons (US), Kilometers/Liters (Metric), or Miles/Liters (UK), you can easily toggle your preferred units in the Calibration settings for seamless, accurate tracking anywhere in the world.
