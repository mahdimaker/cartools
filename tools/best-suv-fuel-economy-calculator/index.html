Frequently Asked Questions About SUVs with the Best Gas Mileage

Everything you need to know about calculating SUV fuel savings, EPA ratings, MPGe, and choosing the right powertrain.

To find the best SUV with good gas mileage, our calculator compares your current vehicle's annual fuel expenditure against each SUV's official EPA energy consumption ratings. We factor in your selected Annual Mileage and current average Fuel/Electricity Rates using the formula:

Annual Cost = (Annual Miles / Combined MPG or MPGe) × Fuel Price per Unit

The 5-year savings represent the difference between your current car's total 5-year fuel cost and the estimated 5-year energy cost of the selected SUV.

Gas SUVs: Top non-hybrid compact SUVs deliver around 28–33 MPG combined.

Hybrid SUVs: Standout models (such as Toyota RAV4 Hybrid and Honda CR-V Hybrid) reach 38–44 MPG combined without needing to plug in.

Plug-in Hybrids (PHEVs) & EVs: Achieve the best fuel consumption overall. MPGe (Miles Per Gallon Equivalent) measures efficiency using the energy in 1 gallon of gas (33.7 kWh), reaching 70 to 120+ MPGe for ultimate efficiency.

For daily commuting, a SUV with great gas mileage generally falls into these benchmarks:

  • Hybrid SUVs (38–44 MPG): Ideal for commuters without home charging who want top tier fuel economy and standard gas convenience.
  • Plug-in Hybrids (PHEVs, 70–90+ MPGe): Perfect if your commute is under 30–40 miles daily, running almost 100% on cheap electric charge with gas backup for long trips.
  • Full EVs (100–125+ MPGe): The absolute lowest cost-per-mile for high-mileage commuters charging at home on off-peak rates.
